Projects

Coast Internet Radio

The website for Coast Internet Radio, a small internet radio station in Newry run by Jim Parr, a family member. Listeners use it to play the live stream and see what's playing now and next. It has been live since May 2026, and I look after it unpaid. In October 2026 I rebuilt it, adding an editor so Jim can change the words, photos, colours and show times himself.

The station's stream is only available over HTTP, and browsers block HTTP audio on an HTTPS page. The site plays it through two small Cloudflare Workers that pass the audio and track information through over HTTPS.

I agree with Jim what listeners need, then build, deploy and maintain the changes. I wrote the first version using online tutorials, my class notes and AI tools, and rebuilt it with Claude Code, which wrote and explained much of the code while I tested, committed and deployed the changes.

Read the Coast case study or visit coastinternetradio.com.
